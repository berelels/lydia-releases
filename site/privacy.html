Lydia

privacy

Lydia runs on your computer. She has no account, no server of her own and no analytics, and she records nothing about you.

What stays on your computer

  • Your voice: it is recorded only while you talk to her, transcribed on your computer (whisper.cpp) and the recording is deleted right after.
  • The clipboard history, in memory only (gone when she quits); passwords and keys are never kept.
  • Your tasks, timers and settings, in her folder (~/.config/lydia).
  • Your calendar is GNOME's own (Evolution Data Server): she reads and changes it there, and keeps no copy.
  • The battery, your USB and Bluetooth devices, the Wi-Fi and the reminders are read on your computer; only today's glasses of water are kept, in her settings folder.
  • Your Gemini key, if you give one, in your system's keyring.
  • The chat is not saved: it disappears when you start another one.
  • The GNOME extension and the KWin script only talk to Lydia, over your session's D-Bus.

What leaves it, only when you use it

  • Chat and the natural voice: what you say or type goes to the engine you picked: Google Gemini (with your key) or Anthropic Claude (with your login). A local Ollama model keeps it on your computer.
  • Weather: Open-Meteo, for the city you type in settings, or a rough one from your internet address (ipwho.is).
  • Lyrics: when the player doesn't send them, the song's title, artist, album and length go to lrclib.net; they can be turned off in Settings.
  • Speed test: Cloudflare. Playing a song on Spotify: a DuckDuckGo search for its link.
  • New versions: once a day, GitHub's page of her latest release. Nothing about you is sent.
  • Installing the voice: the models and packages are downloaded from pypi.org, huggingface.co and github.com when you press "Install".
  • Files in the chat: the images and videos you attach go with your message to that engine, like the text.
  • WhatsApp, only if you turn it on: WhatsApp Web runs inside her, linked with your QR code, and talks only to WhatsApp. Its session stays on your computer; she keeps none of your messages. A document you open from a chat is saved in your Downloads folder.

This site

No cookies and no analytics. The language you pick is remembered in your browser. The fonts come from Google Fonts.
